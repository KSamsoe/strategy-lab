Find an intraday strategy on 15-minute bars that is worth running as a
RISK-ADJUSTED sleeve, not as an index-beater.

THE TARGET. Beat SPY's risk-adjusted performance over this window: buy-and-hold
SPY returned +109.3% at Sharpe 1.36 with a -20.8% max drawdown. Beating that on
Sharpe, with a materially smaller drawdown, is the goal. You are NOT expected to
beat +109.3% in absolute return -- 2023 to 2026 was a strong bull market and an
intraday book paying real costs will very likely return less. Say so plainly
rather than contorting a strategy to chase it. What would make this session a
success is something with a better Sharpe and a shallower drawdown than SPY,
even at half the return. What would make it a failure is a high ratio earned by
sitting in cash: check oos_exposure, and a strategy that is 20% invested and
returns 5% is not a result.

COSTS ARE THE WHOLE GAME HERE, and they are set deliberately unkind: 8bps of
slippage plus $0.005 per share, on every fill. For calibration, the stock momo
strategy re-scaled to 15m returned 36.6% against SPY's 109.3% and paid $3,001
in commission across 2,833 trades at a turnover of 262. Its problem was not the
signal, it was the churn. Every additional round trip has to pay for itself.
Watch `turnover` and the commission line in a review; if turnover is in the
hundreds, the strategy is feeding the broker.

THE EXISTING STRATEGIES ARE TUNED FOR DAILY BARS. momo's `lookback: 126` means
126 *bars*, which at 15m is about five sessions, not six months. Before you
conclude a library strategy does not work intraday, re-scale its windows: one
session is 26 bars, a week is 130, a month is roughly 550. A strategy that looks
broken is usually one whose lookback is off by a factor of 26.

WHAT THE SESSION LOOKS LIKE. Regular hours only, 09:30-16:00 ET, 26 bars per
session, 8 liquid names. There is no overnight bar, so consecutive sessions are
separated by a gap that never appears as a tradeable move -- a strategy holding
overnight is exposed to that gap without being able to react to it. Decide
deliberately whether yours goes flat into the close or carries risk overnight,
and say which in your verdict. Both are legitimate; pretending the gap is not
there is not.

BE HONEST ABOUT THE HOLDOUT. It is about nine months of calendar time. More bars
than a daily study but a SHORTER span, and Sharpe's error bar tracks the span --
roughly plus or minus 1.18 here. Two strategies inside that of each other are
not distinguishable, so lean on full-period behaviour, drawdown and per-period
consistency rather than the fourth decimal of a score.

Finishing with a well-evidenced "nothing here beats holding SPY on a
risk-adjusted basis at these costs" is a genuine result and a good use of the
session. Do not manufacture a winner.
