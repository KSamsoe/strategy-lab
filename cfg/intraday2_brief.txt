Find something that is ACTUALLY INTRADAY and worth running as a risk-adjusted
sleeve. A previous session on this data did not, and reading why is the fastest
way to not repeat it.

WHAT WENT WRONG LAST TIME. Ten runs, eight of them variants of one vol-target
chassis, and the artifact it stood behind traded 22 times in 3.6 years with a
turnover of 0.24. It was a volatility-scaled SPY+QQQ hold: a slow rebalance
evaluated on 15-minute bars, not an intraday strategy. It also had 81% of its
intents clipped by the position cap, so its apparent parameter-insensitivity was
the risk limit, not the strategy. That cap has been raised from 0.25 to 0.50 for
this session, so concentration is now measured rather than filtered -- check
`gate.share_clipped_or_blocked` in a review and treat anything above ~50% as
"what I backtested is not what I wrote".

WHAT COUNTS AS INTRADAY. Your artifact should act on the intraday structure that
only exists at this resolution: the open and close, within-session mean
reversion, the first-hour range, volume or volatility clustering through the
day, or a reaction that decays within hours. As a rough floor, something that
trades fewer than a few hundred times across 915 sessions is not using the
timeframe -- it is a daily strategy wearing 15-minute clothes, and you could
have found it on daily bars in a twentieth of the time. Report trades and
trades-per-session in your verdict. This is a floor, not a target: do not churn
to hit a number, because costs will punish that faster than anything.

EXPLORE MORE THAN ONE IDEA. Last session converged on one family by experiment
three and never left it. You have limited backtests, so spend them on genuinely
different mechanisms rather than on the sixth parameterisation of the first
thing that half-worked. If a family is not showing promise after two runs,
abandon it -- that is a legitimate and often correct move.

THE TARGET. Beat SPY on a risk-adjusted basis: SPY returned +109.3% at Sharpe
1.36 with a -20.8% max drawdown over this window. Beating its Sharpe with a
smaller drawdown is the goal; you are NOT expected to beat +109.3% in absolute
return. But a high ratio bought by sitting in cash is not a result -- watch
`oos_exposure`, and if your edge disappears when you are fully invested, it was
de-levering, not skill.

COSTS ARE THE WHOLE GAME: 8bps slippage plus $0.005 per share on every fill.
Stock momo re-scaled to 15m returned 36.6% against SPY's 109.3% and paid $3,001
in commission across 2,833 trades. Its signal was not the problem; its churn
was. Every round trip must pay for itself.

SCALING: one session is 26 bars, a week is 130, a month is roughly 550. Library
strategies have DAILY-scaled parameters -- momo's `lookback: 126` is five
sessions here, not six months. Re-scale before concluding one does not work.

THE HOLDOUT is about eleven months, and Sharpe's error bar on it is roughly plus
or minus 1.05. Two strategies inside that of each other are not distinguishable;
lean on full-period behaviour, drawdown and per-period consistency.

Finishing with a well-evidenced "nothing genuinely intraday beats holding SPY
risk-adjusted at these costs" is a real result and a good use of the session --
but it only counts if you actually tested intraday mechanics. Last session
reached that conclusion without ever testing one.
